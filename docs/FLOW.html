SwapUp — how a trade-in flows

Two devices at the counter: the store's tablet (iPad or Android tablet, SwapUp Store, signed in for the store) and the customer's phone (SwapUp Check). The advisor never scans anything — the customer scans the tablet's QR. The SwapUp server, GradeIQ and the buyback partner work behind them. Every step below was checked against the code on 16 Oct 2026; steps 4–8 and 13 follow the 17 Oct “Reduce the flow” change: customer found by mobile, one camera pass, the answer by itself, one hand-over confirm.

Once — set up the store

Store staff

Sign up in SwapUp Store

Join with an invite code from your manager (you become an advisor), or register a new store (you become its manager).

Register only works where the programme allows self-sign-up (demo programmes do). Otherwise the programme adds the store from the desk — it is live at once with its credit limit, and step 2 is skipped.
Programme

Approve the store, set its credit limit

Until then the store can test phones and see quotes, but cannot take a trade-in.

Store manager, once

Set the tablet up for the store

Android tablet: the SwapUp Store app. iPad: the browser version (add it to the Home Screen). The manager signs in once and taps Use this device as the store tablet: it stays signed in for the store until a manager removes it. Optional: save the store Wi‑Fi so the tablet shows it as a QR for the customer's phone.

Advisor, every shift

"Who's serving?" — tap your name, type your PIN

4-digit PIN (set the first time with your password). Everything you do is recorded under your name. Switch hands the tablet over; it locks itself after 15 minutes idle.

Customer arrives

Advisor on the tablet

New trade-in → find the customer by mobile

Type the customer's mobile number first. A returning customer's name fills in, with how many trade-ins they have done before and the latest one; a new customer gets a name field. Both are optional. SwapUp opens a case (e.g. SU-AND-00010).

Tablet Customer's phone

Connect: Wi‑Fi QR → test QR → SwapUp Check

The advisor turns the tablet to the customer: the Wi‑Fi QR (if saved), then the test QR and a 6-character code (letters and numbers, valid 30 min). Android: download and open SwapUp Check (Wi‑Fi or mobile data) or use the browser. iPhone: browser only. The customer's phone then leads in three steps — Scan · Test · Done — and is asked nothing about condition.

The model comes from the IMEI: the store enters the IMEI and the model comes from its lookup (GSMA, through Phonecheck); the phone never picks one. With an IMEI on the case the phone is never asked “Which phone is this?” — it is told the IMEI's model, or the store's own. Asked only when no test can tell: “Is this your phone?” for a regional model code on a case with no IMEI and no model (an unknown phone picks it from a list), the storage when the phone can't measure it.

Test — one camera pass, then the phone tests itself

Customer's phone
SwapUp Check

Scan — it shows a code for the tablet

Then, when the camera asks, the white, black and colour screens. It waits there for the camera — the customer is never offered a skip.

Store tablet
SwapUp Store GradeIQ

ONE camera pass: photos + the colour sweep

The tablet's camera app scans the code; GradeIQ's camera page photographs each side and sweeps the screen in one go (“Take photos” opens the same page). GradeIQ's AI grades the glass, body and screen while the phone tests itself.

The camera can't decide (no clear view, its AI off, a grade that isn't final) → “Check the phone” opens inside this step — one tap “All fine — screen OK, body like new” (liquid indicator: can't see) opens the answer, or: the advisor looks at the phone and taps Screen, Body and Liquid indicator at once — one tap each, by name (no colour loop on the customer's phone since 0.9.4; a screen sweep that missed colours adds no screen fault — the advisor's Screen tap decides). “Go on without the camera” (by name) lets a waiting phone move on. An older SwapUp Check (tests first) gets the same single pass after its tests.
Customer's phone (Android app)

Tests — no questions

The battery-health screenshot first, only when the store has no other reading of it (an attached Phonecheck report) — and it can be skipped. Then it checks by itself: battery level, sensors, motion, network and GPS, speaker + mic, earpiece, vibration, flash, cameras; Bluetooth and NFC are checked as present. The customer only swipes every square, plugs in the charger (45 s, else "not tested") and lifts the phone if a camera asks.

Not tested automatically: buttons, multi-touch, fingerprint / face unlock — the advisor checks these.
Server GradeIQ

The answer opens by itself — no “Apply to case”

When the phone's tests and the camera grade are in (and the advisor's taps, where the camera couldn't decide), the server puts the results on the case and the tablet opens the answer. Apply's own rules still decide: it waits (up to 3 minutes after the photos or the screen check, usually under 30 s) while GradeIQ finishes; Samsung model and storage fill in when the model code matches; failed tests add faults; camera damage is added only when it is at least 70% sure — anything weaker is a flag to check by eye. Nothing the camera says sets the price.

Apply refuses (e.g. the case left the counter) → its one-line reason and a Retry. The customer stopped part-way → “Apply the results so far”. Camera AI down (e.g. GradeIQ's AI account out of credit) → its grade comes back failed, the advisor checks the phone by hand, and the SwapUp grade is provisional; the grade report still shows every photo and the colour frames.

The answer — repair or swap up?

Server

Repair price

Samsung India part prices + labour + 18% GST, with warranty / Care+ applied.

No automatic price for liquid, no power, frame or network faults, parts not in the price book, or non-Samsung phones — type the service centre's (or customer's) quote.
Partner

Trade-in price

Never typed in. In this order: PriceIQ's live refurbished-market price at the phone's grade (lowest listing), less the 5%→2% markdown and SwapUp's % per defect — shown with its age when over 14 days old, none when over 90 → the partner's uploaded rate card → otherwise no price: "No market price yet", with the reason.

Advisor

Pick the new phone · bonus worked out

The 2–5% bonus comes from the best running campaign (with any ₹ cap). A manager can change it within that campaign's band, with a reason.

Tablet / desk Customer's phone Server

One answer — the same lines on every screen

Written by the server, word for word: Current grade C · screen cracked · Market price for grade C ₹25,000 · We pay you ₹26,000 (the market price + the SwapUp premium: 5%→2% of it by band) · + ₹6,199 bonus if you exchange with a new device · or repair it for ₹34,096 — Repair and Swap up side by side, with the one thing still missing, if anything — each number once. The customer's phone (app or browser) shows the same lines once the partner's price is in; until then "The advisor is working out your price." The recommendation: repair costs half the new phone's price or more, or as much as the phone is worth repaired → leans upgrade. Repair 20% of a new phone or less, or it adds more value than it costs → leans repair. Both → "either". Covered by warranty / Care+ → repair. The bonus never counts.

Customer accepts

Advisor

“Take the trade-in” — one screen

The IMEI only if the case has none (typed, or scanned off the box) · name and mobile, prefilled · ID type and its last 4 characters (only those are stored) · the government IMEI check (KYM, SMS to 14422) · ONE hand-over confirm, “Owner confirmed · SIM out · IMEI matches · no EMI/lock” — the server still receives and requires all four. A storage the customer typed, or an IMEI that fails its check digit, adds one tick of its own.

Blocks: KYM flags the phone · IMEI already traded in · store not approved · not enough credit left for this trade-in · no trade-in price · quote expired · camera says it's a different phone (until a manager overrides).
Server

Trade-in locked — credit HELD

The market price is held on the store's credit line; the premium and the bonus are the programme's claim. Customer pays new price − we pay − bonus (refused if the new phone costs less). The customer's phone says "Traded in" and stops polling. Print the receipt and device tag.

Tablet Customer's phone

The grade report

"Show to customer" puts it on the tablet as a QR; the customer's phone already shows its SwapUp grade and "See your phone's report". It says what was tested and why the phone got its grade — no money. The partner sees the version frozen at hand-over.

Customer Advisor

Clear the customer's data

The customer signs out of Samsung + Google and factory-resets from Settings (SwapUp Check shows how). The advisor ticks what they saw: setup screen, no old-account prompt. Recorded as "not a certified erasure".
